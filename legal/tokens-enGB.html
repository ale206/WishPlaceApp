Wishplace – Credits Policy

Version: 1.0.0

Last updated: 11 February 2026

This Credits Policy ("Policy") explains the acquisition, management, and usage of digital credits ("Credits") within the Wishplace platform (the "Platform"). This Policy forms an integral part of the Wishplace Terms and Conditions.

1. Purpose of This Policy

Wishplace uses a credit-based system to facilitate specific technical operations within its digital venue. These Credits serve as the internal unit required for users to publish real estate requirements, list properties, or initiate contact with other users via the Platform’s matching environment.

2. Legal Status & Right of Withdrawal

Credits are digital consumable units purchased exclusively through the Platform. They have no cash value and are non-refundable.

EU Consumer Law & Withdrawal Waiver: By purchasing Credits and requesting their immediate allocation to your account, you explicitly consent to the immediate performance of the service and acknowledge that you **waive your statutory 14-day right of withdrawal** once the Credits are added to your account.

By purchasing or using Credits, you acknowledge that:

  • They have no monetary value and are not legal tender.
  • They are not transferable between accounts or users.
  • They cannot be exchanged for cash or real-world currency.
  • They represent a limited, non-exclusive, revocable license to access specific technical functions within Wishplace.

3. Acquisition of Credits

Credits can only be obtained through authorized channels:

  • In-App Purchases: Handled exclusively via the Apple App Store (iOS) or Google Play Store (Android).
  • Payment Processing: Wishplace does not process payments directly. All billing, currency conversion, and payment security are managed by the respective App Store providers.
  • Promotional Credits: Wishplace may, at its sole discretion, award "Bonus" credits. These carry the same restrictions as purchased credits.

4. Usage and Consumption

Credits are "consumed" when used to perform specific operations. Current enabled operations include:

  • Adding a "Wish Place": Publishing specific real estate requirements to the matching algorithm.
  • Adding a "Property": Listing a property for rent or sale.
  • Contacting a User: Initiating a direct communication channel (chat) with a matched party.

The required number of Credits for any action will be clearly displayed before you confirm the operation. Wishplace reserves the right to introduce new technical operations or modify the credit requirements for existing ones at any time.

5. The Confirmation Window & Reimbursement

In accordance with Section 9 of the Terms and Conditions, specific operations feature a temporary "confirmation window."

  • Reimbursement: Credits used for these actions can only be reimbursed if you select "Delete" strictly within the time period allowed by the App's interface.
  • Finality: Once the window closes, Credits are considered consumed. The Operator is not responsible for an inability to cancel due to connectivity issues or device lag.

6. Management and Security

  • Infrastructure: Credit balances are stored and managed via secure PostgreSQL databases hosted on Google Cloud infrastructure.
  • Authentication: Your credit balance is strictly tied to your unique User Identifier (UID) provided by the authentication system (e.g., Apple, Google, or Email login). If you lose access to your provider account, Wishplace may be unable to recover your balance.

7. Refunds and Forfeiture

  • Used Credits: Non-refundable once the digital service has been rendered or the confirmation window has passed.
  • Unused Credits: Generally non-refundable. Because all transactions are processed via In-App Purchases, the Apple App Store and Google Play Store act as the "Merchants of Record." Consequently:
    • Wishplace does not have the technical capacity to issue monetary refunds directly.
    • Any requests for monetary refunds must be submitted to the respective Store (Apple or Google), which retains sole discretion to grant or deny such requests according to their own terms and policies.
  • Account Deletion: If you delete your account, any remaining balance is permanently forfeited.

8. Investigation of Issues & Limitation of Liability

In the event of technical discrepancies, failed operations, or disputes regarding Credit balances:

  • Best Efforts Investigation: Wishplace will, upon receipt of a documented report, make reasonable efforts to investigate the issue and clarify the status of the technical operation or ledger entry.
  • No Ultimate Responsibility: Notwithstanding our efforts to assist, the Operator is not responsible for errors, losses, or transaction failures caused by third-party infrastructure, App Store environments, or user-side connectivity issues. Wishplace does not guarantee that every investigation will result in a Credit restoration or monetary reimbursement.

9. Account Integrity & Illegal Acquisition

Wishplace reserves the right to suspend access or reset credit balances without compensation in cases of:

  • Fraud or Chargebacks: Reversed payments result in immediate removal of Credits and potential account suspension.
  • Illegal Acquisition: Hacking, exploiting software vulnerabilities, or unauthorized manipulation of the Credit system will result in immediate termination of the account and potential legal action.
  • Platform Abuse: Attempts to "farm" or manipulate the system.
